A Rwandan company with a mission

Inyenyeri is a Social Benefit Company. We exist to make clean cooking the normal choice for homes across Rwanda.

Our story

Most Rwandan families still cook on charcoal or open fires. That means smoke indoors, rising fuel costs and forests under pressure.

Inyenyeri was built to change this. We provide modern stoves and clean pellet fuel, serving families in Gisenyi (Rubavu) with real, local support.

Health first

Less smoke means healthier mothers, children and neighbours.

Planet-friendly

Cleaner fuel helps protect forests and reduce emissions.

Built on service

We train, support and stay in touch long after the first purchase.
